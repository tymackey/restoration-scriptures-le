# Privacy Policy for Restoration Scriptures

**Last Updated: April 19, 2025**

## Introduction

Thank you for choosing Restoration Scriptures. This Privacy Policy is designed to help you understand what information we collect and how we use it.

## Information Collection and Use

Restoration Scriptures does not collect any personal information from its users. We do not:

- Request, access, or store personal data
- Track your location
- Access your device's storage, camera, microphone, or other sensors
- Use analytics tools that collect user data
- Serve personalized advertisements based on user data
- Create user accounts that require personal information

## App Permissions

Restoration Scriptures only requires Internet access permission. This permission is solely used to stream scripture audio content and not for collecting or transmitting any user data.

## Third-Party Services

Our app does not integrate with or utilize any third-party services that would collect user information.

## Data Security

Since our app does not collect any user information, there is no user data to secure, store, or transmit.

## Children's Privacy

Our app does not collect information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 or as defined as minors in their respective jurisdictions.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
